# Buying advice

1. **Needs first**: what it is for, must-haves, budget, who uses it, what it must work with (phone ecosystem, monitor ports, router).
2. **Needs to specs**: translate into the few specs that matter (for a laptop: RAM, SSD size, screen, battery, weight; for Wi-Fi: house size, wired backhaul, Wi-Fi 6/7; for a drive: capacity, SSD vs HDD, warranty).
3. **Two or three picks**: a good-enough pick, the best value, and a step-up, each with why.
4. **Prices are dated**: look them up now (web search, store pages, a price tracker such as Keepa or camelcamelcamel) and write "about $X as of YYYY-MM-DD at <store>". Never quote a price from memory.
5. **Check fit**: PC parts through a compatibility checker such as PCPartPicker; ports and sizes against what the owner has.
6. **Reviews**: prefer measured reviews (rtings and similar) over listicles; note the review date.

Warn when a deal looks off (marketplace sellers, refurbished without warranty, old models at new prices). Keep the answer short: a table of picks, then one line on what to avoid.
